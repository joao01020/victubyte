# VICTUBYTE Roadmap

## Vision

VICTUBYTE aims to combine entertainment, programming, logic, cybersecurity and
interactive storytelling into an engaging 2D experience.

The goal is to make players learn through exploration, experimentation,
problem-solving and meaningful decisions.

This roadmap represents the current development direction. Features and
priorities may change.
